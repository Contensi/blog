On 20 October 2026, Proxmox experts and users will gather in Frankfurt am Main for Proxtalks - and Contensi will be there. Moataz Elmasry, Managing Director, and Julius Schlüter, DevOps Engineer for Microsoft Cloud Services, will be on site as points of contact for anything around the Proxmox ecosystem.

## What is Proxtalks?

Proxtalks bills itself, in the organisers’ own words, as "the conference for Proxmox experts and users": a one-day format of talks and panel discussions, organised by Thomas-Krenn.AG together with NETWAYS Event Services GmbH. The programme covers new Proxmox features, storage technologies such as ZFS, Ceph and DRBD/LINSTOR, virtualisation management, high availability, clustering and power supply concepts - topics aimed directly at technical staff and administrators looking to get more out of their virtualisation environment. The event takes place at Westhafen Pier 1 in Frankfurt am Main.

## Contensi on site

This is exactly the right setting for us: Proxmox and Ceph are core to our portfolio, from initial migration advisory through to ongoing operations. If you have questions on site about a VMware migration, Ceph-based storage architectures, or running a Proxmox cluster in practice, Moataz Elmasry and Julius Schlüter are there as direct points of contact - no appointment needed, right there at the event.
